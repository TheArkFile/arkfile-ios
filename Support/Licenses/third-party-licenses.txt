THIRD-PARTY SOFTWARE NOTICES AND LICENSE REFERENCES
===================================================

This document identifies third-party software and assets in the current
ArkFile iOS source line. Direct-package notices are preserved below. The
ArkFile-controlled CoreKiwix build is also mapped component-by-component to
the full notices retained under Dependencies/CoreKiwixNativeNotices in the
matching public source release.

The controlled recipe and notice inventory are present for CoreKiwix
14.2.0+arkfile.1. One verified source-and-relink archive contains its framework,
corresponding source, build inputs, notices, and relink material; it is bound to
the source lock, build manifest, SPDX SBOM, and object map. For external
TestFlight or App Store distribution, that archive is published once per exact
native build in TheArkFile/arkfile-ios and recorded by URL and SHA-256 in each
matching app source release.
